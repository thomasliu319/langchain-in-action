B01使用不当造成损坏 Broken or malfunctions due to personal reasons

【Definition】

The product has been dropped from a height or otherwise improperly used, causing it to no longer work.

【Tips for CS agent】

Generally speaking, our products have undergone quality testing before leaving the manufacturer. Therefore, under normal circumstances, serious failures will not occur.

【Fault Judgement】

1

If the order is less than 3 months - A partial refund (approximately 30%)/replacement device can be provided based on the actual situation.

2

If the order is 6-12 months -

Explain to customers that if product failure is caused by improper use, it is generally not covered by the warranty.

If the customer is dissatisfied and threatens to leave a negative review, we can apply for a partial refund/replacement device depending on the situation.

3

Out of warranty - provide a 10% off code and explain the warranty policy.

If the customer does not accept the discount and is disappointed, you may discuss with the corresponding sales representative whether an exception can be made (replacement/partial refund, etc.).

B01损坏/丢失说明书 The manual is damaged/lost

【Tips for CS agent】

We can download the manual in PDF format according to the model number and send it to the customer.
